Aurum

Your net worth, cards, budget and plan in one private place. It runs on your own computer; nothing is uploaded to anyone.

What it does

One number, then the detail

Net worth across cash, savings, funds, shares, bonds and gold, with history, safe versus growth, and a short list of what needs you this week.

Any bank, any card

Statements are read from e-mailed PDFs or imported from a CSV, whatever the bank, then sorted into categories and trips and checked for what they really cost you in fees and exchange-rate markup.

A plan, not just a ledger

Emergency fund, dated goals, free money to invest, projections and a staged-buying plan, built from your real income and spending.

An assistant that asks first

Chat with Claude or GPT about your own numbers. It can only research and propose; every change is a card you approve.

Any currency

Pick your currency once (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY...). Everything is kept and shown in it, with rates for the rest.

Locked down

Local-only server, strong password, Face ID from your phone, encrypted keys, strict browser protections, tested. Read how.

A look around

On your phone

Install it to your Home Screen and open it with Face ID. Phone guide →

Try it without entering anything

Start the app and press Open the demo instead on the sign-in page. You get a complete, invented year of data (a made-up person, in US dollars) held in a separate file that never touches your real data.

Aurum is a private, single-user app that runs on your own computer. Every screenshot on this site comes from the built-in demo: an invented person, invented banks and merchants, all amounts in US dollars. No real data appears anywhere in this repository. MIT licensed. Not financial advice.
