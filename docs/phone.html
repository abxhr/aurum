Phone

Aurum never listens on the network. Your phone reaches it through Tailscale, which gives your own devices a private HTTPS address, and unlocks with Face ID instead of a password.

One-time setup

  1. Tailscale on both devices. Install it on the computer that runs Aurum and on your iPhone, signed in to the same account. In the Tailscale admin console, turn on MagicDNS and HTTPS certificates.
  2. Publish Aurum privately (on the computer). This forwards your private address to the local server and nothing else: 
    tailscale serve --bg 5000
    Use serve, never funnel: funnel would put it on the public internet.
  3. Restart Aurum (or use the background service). It detects your Tailscale name and remembers it.
  4. Add the iPhone. On the computer: Settings → Security → Add an iPhone. A QR code appears; scan it with the iPhone camera, open the link in Safari and confirm with Face ID. The link works once and expires in ten minutes. The first phone to enrol becomes the owner; anyone else on your tailnet is refused.
  5. Add to Home Screen. Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, and open it from there. (Required on iOS for notifications.)
  6. Alerts (optional). On the phone: Settings → Security → Turn on alerts.

How the phone is treated differently

  • Passwords are never accepted from the phone; only Face ID (a passkey).
  • A phone session locks itself after two idle minutes, so the next tap needs Face ID again, and expires after twelve hours.
  • Remote requests must carry the owner's Tailscale identity, added by Tailscale's own proxy.
  • Lost phone: on the computer, Settings → Security → remove the device, or Sign out all other devices.
